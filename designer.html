CRAFTING
THE Future.

Nova is a multidisciplinary fashion innovator based in digital computational space. My body of work investigates the tactile nature of virtual cloth and the seamless migration of garments across physical bodies and metaverse avatars.

By merging sustainable digital simulation pipelines with high-end archival streetwear silhouettes, Nova Studio functions as both an atelier and an experimental research laboratory establishing the next decade of aesthetic standards.
