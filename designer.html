PORTRAIT NO. 04 ATELIER LONDON
CREATIVE DIRECTION & ATELIER HEAD

Memuna Gandhi.

“Fashion is not an algorithm of trending silhouettes. It is a slow, tactile conversation between the human body, raw fabric, and structural space.”

Trained in traditional pattern drafting and contemporary architectural silhouette construction, Memuna founded NOVA Atelier to push the boundaries of garment volume without compromising wearable human grace.

Working across studios in London and Tokyo, each collection is born from analog muslin draping on dress forms, hands-on textile manipulation, and meticulous tailored finishing.
